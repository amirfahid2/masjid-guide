Masjid Guide

Masjid Guide shows the prayer times of the masjids you pray at, read straight from each masjid's own website, and reminds you before each prayer at your home masjid.

  • Add a masjid by pasting the link to the page on its website that lists the prayer times.
  • Times are read fresh every time the app opens.
  • Choose how many minutes before Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Jumu'ah you want to be alerted.
  • Five looks to choose from, and adjustable text size.
