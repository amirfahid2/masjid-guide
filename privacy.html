Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Masjid Guide ("the app") is made by Pentapillars, LLC. The app is designed to collect nothing beyond what it needs to work.

What stays on your phone

Your list of masjids (names and website links), your home masjid, your alert settings, your chosen look and text size, and the most recently read prayer times are stored only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us.

Network requests the app makes

  • The masjid websites you add. Each time the app opens, it loads the pages you added to read the current prayer times, exactly as a web browser would.
  • Prayer-time lookup. When a masjid's website lists a prayer as "x minutes after adhan" or "after sunset", the app asks a prayer-times service for that day's adhan or sunset time for the masjid's coordinates and adds the minutes. Only the coordinates are sent; nothing identifies you.
  • Address lookup. If you type a city or address for a masjid, or the app reads the address printed on the masjid's website, it is converted to coordinates using Apple's geocoding service.

Location

The app only asks for your location if you tap "Use my current location" when adding a masjid. The resulting coordinates are saved on your phone as that masjid's location and used only for the prayer-time lookup described above. You can clear them at any time in the masjid's settings. The app never tracks your location in the background.

Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification server is involved.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
