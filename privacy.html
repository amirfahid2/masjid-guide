Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Masjid Guide ("the app") is made by Pentapillars, LLC. The app is designed to collect nothing beyond what it needs to work.

What stays on your phone

Your list of masjids (names and website links), your home masjid, your alert settings, your chosen look and text size, and the prayer times the app has read are stored only on your device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a copy of your home masjid's times from the same device storage. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us.

Network requests the app makes

  • The masjid websites you add. Early each morning, and when you open the app if the morning read did not happen, the app loads the pages you added to read the current prayer times, exactly as a web browser would. Nothing is kept between loads: no cookies, no browsing history.
  • Prayer-time lookup. When a masjid's website lists a prayer as "x minutes after adhan" or "after sunset", the app asks a prayer-times service for that day's adhan or sunset time for the masjid's coordinates and adds the minutes. Only approximate coordinates (rounded to about a kilometre) are sent; nothing identifies you.
  • Address lookup. If you type a city or address for a masjid, or the app reads the address printed on the masjid's website, it is converted to coordinates using Apple's geocoding service.

Location

The app only asks for your location if you tap "Use my current location" when adding a masjid. The resulting coordinates are saved on your phone as that masjid's location and used only for the prayer-time lookup described above. You can clear them at any time in the masjid's settings. The app never tracks your location in the background.

Donate button

When a masjid's website has a donation page, the app shows a Donate button that opens that page in your browser. The app never handles payments and never sees what you give.

Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled locally on your phone. No notification server is involved.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
