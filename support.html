Adding a masjid

Swipe past your last masjid on the main page to the Add Masjid card, or open Settings (gear, top right) → Masjids → Add masjid. Search for your masjid by name (search is new and covers a few masjids so far), or paste the link to the page on the masjid's website that shows the prayer times. If the times live on their own page, such as "Prayer Times", use that link rather than the home page. The app reads the page and fills in the masjid's name, which you can change. If no times are found, the app asks before adding it, and the masjid's page offers "Change the link" and "Try again".

The main page

Under the date you'll see how long until the next prayer, for example "Asr in 1h 12m". On Fridays, Jumu'ah takes Dhuhr's place; on other days it is shown faded with "Fridays" under it. Tap "Refresh" at the bottom, or pull down, to read the masjid's site again. Swipe sideways to move between masjids; the dots at the bottom show where you are and the plus is the Add Masjid card.

Your home masjid

The house icon at the top left marks your home masjid. Alerts and widgets follow it. To change it, swipe to another masjid and tap the house, or in Settings → Masjids touch and hold a masjid and choose "Make home masjid".

When the times are read

Each morning the app reads every masjid's website in the background, so today's times are already there when you open it. If the morning read didn't happen (for example the phone was off), the app reads the site when you open it.

"Haven't been updated in at least 15 days"

If a masjid's website has shown exactly the same times for 15 or more days, a small note appears at the bottom of that masjid's page. Many masjids keep fixed iqamah times for weeks, so the note is only a reminder to check with the masjid. It disappears on its own as soon as the times change.

Alerts

The first time your home masjid's times load, the app offers to remind you 15 minutes before each prayer with one tap. To change it later: Settings → Alerts. "All daily prayers" turns all five on or off, "Remind me" sets one time for all of them, and each prayer below can have its own time. Jumu'ah alerts come on Fridays only. When the website lists the adhan, "Count down to" lets reminders count down to the adhan instead of the prayer. Notifications must be allowed for Masjid in the iPhone Settings app; the Alerts page has a button that takes you there if they're off. Opening the app keeps the next twelve days of alerts scheduled; if you haven't opened it for a while, a quiet note reminds you the day before they run out.

Widgets

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and look for Masjid. There are three: a square countdown, a square with bigger numbers, and a wider one with all of today's prayers. Widgets use the look you picked in the app; to give one a dark or light background instead, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, then tap the space under the clock and pick Masjid. There's a strip with the countdown and a round one that fills as the prayer gets closer. Tap the date above the clock to add a one-line version there.

Siri and Shortcuts

Say "Next prayer in Masjid" to hear the next prayer at your home masjid and how long until it. The same action is in the Shortcuts app.

Reordering and removing masjids

Settings → Masjids → Edit lets you drag masjids into a new order; the main page follows it. Swipe left on a masjid, or touch and hold it, to remove it. The app asks first, and tells you which masjid becomes home if you remove the home masjid.

"Needs location" or "minutes after adhan"

Some masjids list a prayer as "x minutes after adhan" or "after sunset". To turn that into a clock time the app needs the masjid's location. The app tries to read the address printed on the website; otherwise tap the note on the masjid's page, or open the masjid in Settings → Masjids, and type its city or address or tap "Use my current location".

Looks, text size and the next prayer

Settings → Appearance: swipe through the pictures and tap the look you want. "Next prayer" shows a small preview of each way the coming prayer can stand out. Text size changes the size of the times on the main page, and they also grow with the text size set for your iPhone. Masjid works on iPad too.

Adhan times

When a masjid's website lists the adhan as well as the iqama, Settings → Appearance → "Show adhan times" shows the adhan in smaller type under each prayer time.

Donate

If a masjid's website has a Donate page, a Donate button appears at the bottom of that masjid's page and opens the website in your browser. Donations happen on the masjid's own site; the app never handles payments. To point the button at a different page, open the masjid in Settings → Masjids and fill in "Donate link".
