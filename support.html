Adding a masjid

Open Settings (gear, top right) → Add masjid. Paste the link to the page on the masjid's website that shows the prayer times. If the times live on their own page (for example a "Prayer Times" page), use that link rather than the home page. Tap "Check the website" to see what the app found before saving.

"Needs location" or "minutes after adhan"

Some masjids list a prayer as "x minutes after adhan" or "after sunset". To turn that into a clock time the app needs the masjid's location. The app tries to read the address printed on the website; otherwise open the masjid in Settings and type its city or address, or tap "Use my current location".

Alerts

Settings → Alerts: turn on each prayer you want and set how many minutes before. Alerts follow the home masjid (the house icon at the top left of the main page). Jumu'ah alerts fire on Fridays only. Notifications must be allowed for Masjid Guide in the iPhone Settings app.

Looks, text size and the next prayer

Settings → Style: swipe through the pictures and tap the one you want. Settings → Text size changes the size of the times on the main page. Settings → Next prayer chooses how the upcoming prayer stands out.

Adhan times

When a masjid's website lists the adhan as well as the iqama, Settings → "Show adhan times" shows the adhan in smaller type under each prayer time, and "Alert at" chooses whether reminders count down to the adhan or to the prayer.

Donate

If a masjid's website has a Donate page, a Donate button appears at the bottom of that masjid's page and opens the website in your browser. Donations happen on the masjid's own site; the app never handles payments. To point the button at a different page, open the masjid in Settings and fill in "Donate link".
