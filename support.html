Adding a masjid

Swipe past your last masjid on the main page to the Add Masjid card, or open Settings (gear, top right) → Add masjid. Search for your masjid by name (search is in beta, so only a few masjids are listed so far), or paste the link to the page on the masjid's website that shows the prayer times. If the times live on their own page (for example a "Prayer Times" page), use that link rather than the home page. The app reads the page and fills in the masjid's name, which you can change.

When the times are read

Each morning the app reads every masjid's website in the background, so today's times are already there when you open it. If the morning read didn't happen (for example the phone was off), the app reads the site when you open it. Pull down on a masjid's page to read its site again right away.

"Haven't been updated in at least 5 days"

If a masjid's website has shown exactly the same times for 5 or more days, a small note appears at the bottom of that masjid's page. Many masjids keep fixed iqamah times for weeks, so the note is only a reminder to check with the masjid. It disappears on its own as soon as the times change.

Home Screen widget

The Masjid widget is a small square that counts down to the next prayer at your home masjid and shows the prayer and its time. To add it, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit (top left), tap Add Widget, and look for Masjid. There are two squares to choose from: the regular one and one with numbers twice the size (it drops the seconds while hours remain, so the numbers stay big). The widget uses the same look you picked in Settings, and it follows the masjid marked with the house icon.

There is also a Lock Screen version. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Lock Screen, tap the space under the clock, and pick Masjid.

"Needs location" or "minutes after adhan"

Some masjids list a prayer as "x minutes after adhan" or "after sunset". To turn that into a clock time the app needs the masjid's location. The app tries to read the address printed on the website; otherwise open the masjid in Settings and type its city or address, or tap "Use my current location".

Alerts

Settings → Alerts: turn on each prayer you want and set how many minutes before. Alerts follow the home masjid (the house icon at the top left of the main page). Jumu'ah alerts fire on Fridays only. Notifications must be allowed for Masjid Guide in the iPhone Settings app.

Looks, text size and the next prayer

Settings → Style: swipe through the pictures and tap the one you want. Settings → Text size changes the size of the times on the main page. Settings → Next prayer chooses how the upcoming prayer stands out.

Adhan times

When a masjid's website lists the adhan as well as the iqama, Settings → "Show adhan times" shows the adhan in smaller type under each prayer time, and "Alert at" chooses whether reminders count down to the adhan or to the prayer.

Donate

If a masjid's website has a Donate page, a Donate button appears at the bottom of that masjid's page and opens the website in your browser. Donations happen on the masjid's own site; the app never handles payments. To point the button at a different page, open the masjid in Settings and fill in "Donate link".
